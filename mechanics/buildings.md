# Buildings

Immovable structures. No code execution (except turrets, which are separate).

## Harvester

- **Cost:** 80 Ti | **HP:** 30 | **Scale:** +10%
- Must be built on an ore tile (titanium or axionite). Automatically harvests and outputs resources to adjacent conveyors.

## Foundry

- **Cost:** 120 Ti | **HP:** 50 | **Scale:** +100%
- Accepts raw axionite + titanium, produces refined axionite.
- Extremely expensive in scaling — build sparingly.

## Road

- **Cost:** 1 Ti | **HP:** 10 | **Scale:** +0.5%
- Walkable surface for builder bots. Cheapest way to create paths.

## Barrier

- **Cost:** 3 Ti | **HP:** 30 | **Scale:** +1%
- Blocks pathing. Use defensively.
