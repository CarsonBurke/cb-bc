# Conveyors & Transport

Transport resources across the map. Each can hold one resource stack.

## Conveyor

- Accepts from 3 non-output sides, outputs in facing direction.
- **Cost:** 3 Ti | **HP:** 20 | **Scale:** +1%

## Splitter

- Accepts from behind only. Alternates output across 3 front directions, prioritizing least-recently-used direction.
- **Cost:** 6 Ti | **HP:** 20 | **Scale:** +1%

## Bridge

- Teleports resources to a specific target tile within r²≤9, chosen at build time.
- Accepts input from all sides.
- **Cost:** 10 Ti | **HP:** 20 | **Scale:** +1%

## Armoured Conveyor

- Same as basic conveyor but 50 HP.
- **Cost:** 10 Ti + 5 Ax | **HP:** 50 | **Scale:** +1%
